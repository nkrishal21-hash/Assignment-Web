Welcome to Goldstar Shoes

Goldstar is one of the most popular and trusted shoe brands in Nepal. Manufactured by Kiran Shoes, the brand has been providing high-quality, durable, and affordable shoes for many years. Goldstar shoes became especially popular in the 1990s because of their strong build, simple design, and low price. They quickly became a household name in Nepal, as people of all ages—from students to workers—started wearing them for everyday use. The shoes are known for their comfort, long-lasting quality, and ability to handle rough use, which made them reliable for daily wear, school, work, and even sports activities. Over the years, Goldstar has expanded its range to include various types of shoes to meet the needs of different customers. Their products include classic shoes, casual sneakers, sports and running shoes, and school shoes. The classic Goldstar G10 model is particularly famous for its durability and simple style, making it one of the best-selling shoes in Nepal. The sports and running shoes are lightweight and comfortable, designed for physical activities and daily exercise. Meanwhile, casual sneakers are designed for style-conscious users, offering a combination of comfort and modern designs. School shoes are specifically made to match school uniforms while providing comfort and satisfaction for long hours of wear.
